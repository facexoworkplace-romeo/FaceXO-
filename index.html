About FaceXO

FaceXO is a social networking and digital community platform developed by FaceXO Innovations Limited. It brings social interaction, content sharing, messaging, video entertainment, marketplace activities and community connections together in one application.

What FaceXO Offers

  • Social feeds for sharing posts and interacting with other users.
  • Short-form video content and entertainment.
  • Private messaging and communication features.
  • Marketplace features for discovering products and connecting with sellers.
  • Live streaming and interactive content.
  • Adult dating and relationship features restricted to eligible adults.

Google Sign-In

FaceXO uses Google Sign-In to allow users to securely authenticate and access their accounts. Google account information obtained through authentication is used for account access, account identification and related functions disclosed in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell Google user data.

Responsible Use of AI

FaceXO does not use Google APIs to generate or facilitate AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery. FaceXO prohibits the creation, sharing or distribution of non-consensual intimate imagery and content that violates our safety policies.
