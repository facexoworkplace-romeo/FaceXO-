1. Summary

This summary provides an overview of our privacy practices. The full policy below explains them in more detail.

  • We collect information you provide, information generated when you use FaceXO, and limited information from service providers.
  • You can register using email or phone authentication or choose Google Sign-In.
  • Google Sign-In provides basic account information, including your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile photograph where available.
  • Google Sign-In information is used to authenticate your account and provide account-related functionality.
  • We do not use Google Sign-In data for advertising.
  • Camera, microphone and location permissions are used when you enable features that require them.
  • FaceXO uses Google AdMob to deliver and measure advertisements.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • FaceXO does not currently process payments within the app.
  • Dating features are optional and restricted to adults aged 18 and above.
  • You can request deletion of your FaceXO account and associated personal information.

2. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FaceXO Innovations Limited ("FaceXO", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, protects and manages personal information when you use the FaceXO mobile application, website and related services ("Services").

FaceXO is a social networking and digital platform offering profiles, content sharing, private messaging, one-to-one voice and video calls, video sharing, live broadcasting, a marketplace, entertainment and optional dating and compatibility features.

Users may register using email or phone authentication or choose Google Sign-In. Google Sign-In is optional.

By creating an account or using FaceXO, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the processing described in this Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request it separately.

3. Who Is Responsible for Your Information

FaceXO Innovations Limited is responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal information through the Services, subject to applicable law.

Our service providers process information on our behalf for the purposes described in this Policy, or may act as independent controllers where their own privacy policies apply.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia, and other relevant laws in countries where our users are located.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, phone number, authentication credentials and registration details.
  • Profile Information: Profile photographs, biography, gender, interests and preferences.
  • User-Generated Content: Posts, comments, reactions, photographs, videos, voice notes and other uploaded content.
  • Communication Information: Information associated with messages, voice calls and video calls.
  • Marketplace Information: Listings, descriptions, photographs, seller information and buyer enquiries.
  • Dating Information: Dating preferences, relationship intentions, compatibility preferences and dating interactions.
  • Verification Information: Materials provided during identity, phone number or email verification. Identity verification may involve an identity document image and selfie or liveness check.

4.2 Google Sign-In Information

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides FaceXO with basic Google account information in accordance with the permissions presented during authorization.

This information may include:

4.3 Automatically Collected Information

4.4 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from service providers supporting authentication, identity verification, hosting, messaging, video delivery, advertising, mapping and security.

5. Camera, Microphone and Media

FaceXO does not intentionally record or store the content of one-to-one voice or video calls. Technical connection information is processed by the provider supporting the call.

11. Google User Data and Google Sign-In

This section specifically explains how FaceXO accesses, uses, stores, shares and deletes information received through Google Sign-In.

It applies when you voluntarily choose Google Sign-In. It does not mean FaceXO accesses your entire Google Account.

11.1 Google Account Information We Access

When you choose Google Sign-In, FaceXO uses basic identity permissions requested through the Google authorization flow.

These permissions are commonly represented by the OpenID Connect scopes:

  • openid
  • email
  • profile

Information returned may include:

  • Google Account Identifier: A unique identifier used to recognise and authenticate the Google account.
  • Email Address: The email address associated with the Google account, including verification status where provided.
  • Basic Profile Information: The user's name and profile photograph where returned.
  • Authentication Information: Authentication responses and tokens handled by the sign-in SDK and Firebase Authentication.

The exact fields available depend on the Google account, authorization response and scopes configured in the live application.

FaceXO does not request Google Drive, Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Calendar or other Google content access as part of the basic Google Sign-In functionality described here.

11.2 Why We Use Google User Data

FaceXO uses Google Sign-In information only to provide and support the sign-in and account functionality requested by the user, including:

  • Authenticating users who choose Google Sign-In.
  • Creating FaceXO accounts.
  • Associating a Google sign-in identity with an existing FaceXO account where supported and requested.
  • Obtaining basic name, email address and profile photograph information to populate relevant account fields where returned and used by the application.
  • Maintaining authenticated sessions.
  • Protecting accounts against unauthorised access.
  • Providing account support and troubleshooting sign-in problems.

We do not use Google Sign-In data for:

  • Advertising or ad targeting.
  • Retargeting or personalised advertising.
  • Creditworthiness or lending decisions.
  • Data brokerage or selling personal information.
  • Purposes unrelated to providing or improving FaceXO's user-facing sign-in and account functionality.

11.3 Where Google User Data Is Stored and Processed

Google Sign-In authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.

Firebase processes the authentication response and maintains the authentication identity needed to sign the user in.

FaceXO's application database, powered by Supabase, may store relevant FaceXO account profile fields, such as:

  • User name.
  • Email address.
  • Profile photograph URL.
  • Relevant account association or identifier where needed to operate the account.

Authentication tokens and credentials are handled by the authentication SDK and related authentication infrastructure for sign-in and session management.

FaceXO does not use Google Sign-In tokens to access unrelated Google services or content. We do not intentionally expose authentication credentials to other users.

Google account information may be processed by Google/Firebase and stored in FaceXO's application systems as described above. Processing or storage may occur outside your country of residence, as explained in Section 18.

11.4 How Google User Data Is Shared

FaceXO does not sell, rent or trade Google user data.

We do not disclose Google Sign-In information to advertising networks, data brokers or information resellers. We do not provide Google Sign-In information to Google AdMob for advertising or ad personalisation.

We allow processing of the minimum information necessary with the following providers:

  • Google and Firebase: Process the Google authentication flow and Firebase Authentication account identity.
  • Supabase: Stores relevant FaceXO profile fields and account data where needed for the application's database and account functionality.
  • Operational and security support: Authorised personnel or service providers may access relevant information only when reasonably necessary to maintain the service, investigate security incidents, provide support or comply with law, subject to appropriate confidentiality and access controls.

We do not otherwise transfer Google user data to third parties. Any exceptional disclosure required by law or necessary to protect users or the public will be limited to what is necessary and permitted by applicable policies.

11.5 Google User Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect Google user data, including:

  • Encrypted transmission using HTTPS and TLS.
  • Access controls.
  • Authentication protections.
  • Restricted access to production systems.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Confidentiality and access restrictions for authorised personnel.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11.6 Retention and Deletion of Google User Data

We retain Google-derived account information only while it is needed to provide and secure the user's FaceXO account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent fraud and abuse.

If a user requests deletion of their FaceXO account, associated Google-derived profile information and the account association in our active application systems are handled under the account deletion process in Section 16.

Our stated deletion process includes a 30-day period during which a deletion request may be cancelled, followed by deletion from active systems targeted within a further 60 days.

Backup copies are removed in the normal backup cycle. Information that must be retained for legal or security reasons may be retained for the limited purpose and period required.

Users may also remove FaceXO's access from their Google Account connections page:

Google Account Connections

Revoking Google access prevents future Google-authorised access as applicable, but does not by itself delete the user's FaceXO account or information already stored by FaceXO.

To delete your FaceXO account and associated information, use the in-app deletion process or contact us using the details in Section 23.

11.7 Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

FaceXO's access to and use of information received through Google APIs and Google Sign-In is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Our use is limited to providing or improving user-facing features that are visible and prominent in FaceXO.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it except as permitted by Google's policy, including where necessary to provide or improve the requested functionality, with user consent where required, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law or in another circumstance expressly permitted by that policy.

We request only the Google permissions needed for the sign-in functionality described in this section.

We will update this Policy and provide any required notice or obtain consent if our Google data access or use materially changes.

16. Account Deletion and Data Removal

You can request deletion of your FaceXO account in the app or through an account deletion webpage, where available.

When you request deletion, your account is deactivated and scheduled for deletion. You have 30 days to cancel the request by logging back in.

During this period, your profile and content are not visible to other users.

If you do not cancel, we begin permanently deleting your account and personal information from active systems after the 30-day cancellation period. We aim to complete this within a further 60 days.

Our target is therefore approximately 90 days from the deletion request to deletion from active systems.

This target does not include information we are permitted or required to retain, or copies held in backup systems, which are removed during the normal backup cycle.

Deletion includes Google-derived profile information and the association between your Google sign-in identity and your FaceXO account in our active systems, subject to lawful retention exceptions.

Revoking access from your Google Account is separate and does not itself delete your FaceXO account.

We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, protect security, resolve disputes, establish or defend legal claims, or enforce our policies.

Information already shared with others, such as messages or screenshots saved by recipients, may remain with them.

17. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to provide the Services, maintain your account, comply with law, resolve disputes and keep the platform secure.

  • Account and profile information: While your account is active, and then under the deletion process in Section 16.
  • Google Sign-In information: While needed to authenticate and maintain your FaceXO account, and then under the deletion process in Section 16.
  • Authentication records: For the period needed to maintain account security and meet legal or operational requirements.
  • Identity verification materials: For as long as needed to complete and record verification, subject to provider policies and applicable law.
  • Technical logs and security records: For a limited period needed for security, troubleshooting and fraud prevention.
  • Information needed for legal reasons: For the period required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

When information is no longer needed, we delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it.

Backup copies are removed in accordance with our normal backup retention cycle.
