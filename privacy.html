The full policy below is what applies. • We collect the information you give us, information generated when you use FaceXO, and limited information from our service providers. • Camera, microphone and location are used only when you enable them, and you can turn them off in your device settings. • FaceXO shows ads, served by Google AdMob. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how FaceXO Innovations Limited ("FaceXO", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, protects and manages personal information when you use the FaceXO mobile application, website and related services (the "Services"). FaceXO is a social networking and digital platform offering profiles, content sharing, private messaging, one-to-one voice and video calls, video sharing, live broadcasting, a marketplace, entertainment and an optional dating and compatibility feature. By creating an account or using FaceXO, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the processing of personal information described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain your consent separately before carrying out the relevant processing. You may withdraw consent where applicable, although doing so may affect your ability to use certain features. 3. Who Is Responsible for Your Information FaceXO Innovations Limited is the data controller for personal information processed through the Services. The third-party providers in Section 10 act as processors on our behalf, or as independent controllers where their own terms say so. We handle personal information in line with applicable data protection laws, including the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia, and, where relevant, the laws of the countries where our users are located. 4. document image and a selfie or liveness check. • Support Information: information you provide when contacting support, reporting content, making complaints or taking part in surveys. 4.2 Information Collected Automatically • Device type, manufacturer, operating system and device identifiers (including advertising identifiers). • Application version, crash reports, diagnostics and performance data. • IP address and network information. • Login activity, access times and technical logs. • Feature usage and interactions with content, including videos viewed and engagement. • Account activity, connections and interactions. • Approximate or precise location, where you have enabled location permission (see Section 6). • Ad interaction information, such as ads shown and clicked (see Camera, Microphone and Media FaceXO asks for access to your camera and microphone only when you use features that need them, such as taking profile photographs, recording videos or voice notes, making one-to-one voice or video calls, and broadcasting live video. You can manage or withdraw these permissions at any time in your device settings. Turning them off may stop related features from working. FaceXO does not record or store the content of one-to-one voice or video calls. Technical connection data needed to run the call is processed by our provider (see LiveKit in Section 10). 6. Location Information If you enable location permission, FaceXO may process your approximate or precise location to provide: • nearby user discovery; • location-based recommendations and marketplace results; • distance display between users; • other location-dependent features. Maps and location display are supported by Google Maps (see Section 10). You can turn location permission off at any time in your device settings. You do not need to enable location to use every feature of FaceXO. We do not show your precise location to other users. Where distance is displayed, it is shown as an approximate distance. 7. How We Use Your Information We use your information to: • create, manage and secure user accounts and authenticate users; • provide social networking, messaging, calling and live broadcasting; • let users publish, share and discover content; • operate marketplace listings and buyer-seller enquiries; • provide dating, matching and discovery features for eligible adults; • show maps, nearby results and distances; • personalise feeds, recommendations and discovery; • show advertising and measure its performance; • improve the reliability, performance and functionality of FaceXO; • provide customer support and respond to complaints; • detect fraud, spam, abuse and unauthorised access; • moderate content and enforce our policies; • send account notifications and service communications; • comply with legal obligations; and • protect the rights, safety and security of FaceXO and its users. We only use information for purposes consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law. 8. Advertising (Google AdMob) FaceXO is supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to show ads in the app, including between posts in your feed and within Reels. • What AdMob may collect: device information, advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, app usage and ad interaction data, so that ads can be delivered, measured and protected against fraud. • Personalised and non-personalised ads: depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised based on your interests and activity, or non-personalised. • Your choices: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (for example, Android and iOS advertising settings). Where required, we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. • No sale of your data: we do not sell your personal information. We do not share your private messages, call content or dating profile information with advertisers. • Google's practices: Google processes advertising data under its own terms and privacy policy. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. If you are under 18, we do not show you personalised ads. 9. How Information Is Shared on FaceXO Depending on your privacy settings and the features you use, the following may be visible to other users: profile information and photographs; public posts, comments and reactions; uploaded photographs and videos; marketplace listings and seller information; content shared in live broadcasts; and information on your dating profile. 9.1 Private Communications Messages, voice notes and calls are intended for their recipients. We and our providers process the information needed to deliver, secure and maintain these services. Recipients can save, copy, screenshot or share what you send outside FaceXO. 9.2 Dating Privacy Dating features are separate from your ordinary social activity. We do not automatically publish your dating preferences, matches or dating interactions to your social feed. Your dating profile and interactions may be visible to other eligible adult users within the dating feature, depending on its settings. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information unnecessarily. 9.3 Live Broadcasting FaceXO supports live video broadcasting, which is delivered through YouTube. YouTube may process viewer and broadcaster information under its own terms and privacy policy. Anyone who views a broadcast can see what you share, and viewers may record or capture it outside FaceXO. Avoid broadcasting private or sensitive information. We may monitor live broadcasts to enforce our policies. 10. Third-Party Service Providers We use the following providers to run FaceXO. Each processes information under its own terms and privacy policy, and we take reasonable steps to use providers that maintain appropriate safeguards. 10.1 Supabase: Database and Application Data Supabase is our primary application database. It stores user profiles, posts and feeds, likes and reactions, comments and interactions, follower and following relationships, and other application data needed to operate FaceXO. 10.2 Firebase: Authentication, Messaging and Notifications Firebase supports registration, sign-in, phone number and email verification, chat and message delivery, and push notifications. It may process authentication identifiers, verification information, messages, device tokens and technical information. 10.3 Cloudflare R2: Media Storage Cloudflare R2 stores user-uploaded media such as profile photographs, images, videos, voice notes and other files, and delivers it through associated content delivery infrastructure. 10.4 Didit: Identity Verification Where identity verification is requested, Didit processes the information and materials you provide, which may include an identity document image and a selfie or liveness check, which can involve facial or biometric data. We ask for your explicit consent before verification begins. 10.5 LiveKit: Voice and Video Calls LiveKit supports real-time one-to-one voice and video calling. Audio, video and technical connection information are processed to provide the call. FaceXO does not currently offer group calling. 10.6 YouTube: Live Broadcasting FaceXO uses YouTube to deliver live broadcasts to viewers. When you go live, your video, audio and related streaming and technical information are sent to YouTube, which is operated by Google. YouTube processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). FaceXO does not currently offer interactive multi-guest live streaming. 10.7 Google Maps: Maps and Location Features Google Maps supports map display, nearby discovery and distance features. When you use these features, Google may process your location, device information and IP address under Google's own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). 10.8 Google AdMob: Advertising Google AdMob delivers and measures ads, as described in Section 8. 10.9 Changes to Providers We may add or change providers as FaceXO develops. Before adding a provider that handles sensitive information, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, ask for your consent. 11. Marketplace and Payments FaceXO's marketplace lets users discover products, publish listings, browse items and communicate with sellers or buyers. At first launch, FaceXO does not accept, collect or process payments in the app. We do not hold funds for buyers or sellers, and we do not collect card or mobile money details. Any arrangement or transaction between users takes place outside FaceXO and is between those users. Be careful when arranging transactions with people you do not know, meet in safe public places, and do not pay in advance to someone you cannot trust. If we introduce in-app payments in future, we will update this Privacy Policy before the feature goes live to explain what payment information is collected, which payment providers process it and how it is protected. 12. Account Deletion and Data Removal You can request deletion of your account in the app or through our account deletion webpage. When you request deletion, your account is deactivated and scheduled for deletion. You have 30 days to cancel the request by logging back in. During this time, your profile and content are not visible to other users. If you do not cancel, we begin permanently deleting your account and personal information from our active systems after the 30 days, and we aim to complete this within 60 days. Our maximum target is therefore approximately 90 days from your deletion request to the deletion of your information from our active systems. During this time, your information is not accessible to other users. This target does not include information we are permitted or required to keep (see below) or copies held in backup systems, which are removed in the normal backup cycle. We may keep some information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud and protect security, resolve disputes, establish or defend legal claims, or enforce our policies. Where we keep information, we limit its use to that purpose. Information already shared with others, such as messages you sent, or screenshots and copies they saved, may remain with them. Deactivating or suspending an account is not the same as deleting it. 16. How Long We Keep Information We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the Services, maintain your account, comply with the law, resolve disputes and keep the platform secure. • Account and profile information: while your account is active, and deleted under the process in Section 15 after a deletion request. • Identity verification materials: kept only as long as needed to complete and record verification, then deleted or retained by Didit in line with its own policy and applicable law. • Technical logs and security records: kept for a limited period needed for security and fraud prevention. • Information needed for legal reasons: kept for the period the law requires. When information is no longer needed, we delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it. 17.
