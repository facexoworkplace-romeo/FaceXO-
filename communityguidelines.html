FaceXO Community Guidelines Company: FaceXO Innovations Limited Application: FaceXO Effective Date: 20 November 2026 Last Updated: 20 November 2026 Our Community Promise FaceXO is a place to connect, share, discover and have fun. These Community Guidelines explain what is and is not allowed so that everyone can feel safe and respected. They form part of our Terms of Service, and breaking them can lead to action on your content or account, as explained in Section 10. These Guidelines apply to everything on FaceXO, including profiles, posts, comments, messages, voice notes, calls, Reels, live broadcasts, Marketplace listings, dating profiles and advertisements. They apply to everyone, including public figures and verified accounts. We may update these Guidelines as FaceXO grows. If a situation is not covered here, we will use judgement guided by safety, respect for people and the law. The basics, in short: • Treat others with respect, and accept a “no”. • Protect children. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material or anything that endangers minors. • Keep adult material out of public areas and away from under-18s. • Be who you say you are. No scams, impersonation or fake accounts. • Only sell goods that are legal and genuine. • Respect other people’s work and rights. • Do not game the platform with spam or fake engagement. • Report problems, and use blocking tools whenever you need to. 1. Respectful Behaviour and Harassment FaceXO is for positive connection. Disagreement is normal, but abuse is not. Not allowed: • Harassing, bullying, stalking, insulting or repeatedly targeting a person, including by creating new accounts to contact someone who has blocked you. • Threats of violence, harm, blackmail or intimidation, including threats to share private or intimate material. • Hate speech, meaning content that attacks, dehumanises or encourages violence or discrimination against people because of race, ethnicity, nationality, tribe, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or other protected characteristics. • Content that promotes, praises or supports violence, terrorism, violent extremism or criminal groups. • Graphic violence or cruelty shown for shock value, including content that glorifies serious injury, death or animal abuse. • Encouraging or promoting suicide, self-harm or eating disorders, or mocking people who are struggling. • Doxxing, which means publishing someone’s private information such as home address, phone number, identity documents or private photos without permission. • Coordinated attacks, pile-ons or organising others to harass a person. • Recording, photographing or broadcasting people in private settings without their consent where consent is required. • Sharing false information likely to cause real-world harm, such as dangerous health claims or content designed to mislead people about public safety. Legitimate criticism, journalism, education, research and public-interest discussion are not automatically considered harassment or harmful misinformation. However, such content must still comply with these Guidelines and applicable law. If you are struggling: if you or someone you know may be at risk of harming themselves, please contact local emergency services or a trusted person right away. Content that expresses distress is not banned, but we may respond with support or restrict content that promotes self-harm. What we encourage: be kind, give people space, use mute and block tools, and report abuse rather than responding in kind. 2. Child Safety and Protection of Minors Protecting children is our highest priority. FaceXO is for people aged 13 and above, or any higher minimum age required by applicable local law, and we do not tolerate anything that puts a child at risk. Absolutely prohibited, with zero tolerance: • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, including images, videos, drawings, text or links. • Sexualising minors, including sexual comments about, or sexualised images or videos of, anyone under 18. • Grooming, which means befriending or manipulating a minor to prepare them for sexual contact, exploitation or meeting in person. • Asking minors for sexual content, private photos or private meetings, or sending sexual content to minors. • Trafficking, exploiting or abusing children, including child labour and forced marriage. • Sharing or asking for identifying information about minors in ways that endanger them. • Helping a minor access adult-only features, or misrepresenting age to access them. What we do: we remove such content as a priority, may preserve information, permanently suspend accounts, and report suspected child sexual abuse material and child exploitation to the relevant authorities. We do not warn first for the most serious violations. Reports involving suspected child sexual exploitation, grooming or immediate danger to a minor will receive priority review. FaceXO may restrict accounts, remove content, preserve relevant information where legally permitted and refer suspected offences to appropriate law-enforcement or child-protection authorities. Age rules on FaceXO: • You must be at least 13 to use FaceXO, or meet any higher minimum age required by applicable local law. • The dating features are restricted to adults aged 18 and above, and under-18s must not access them. • We do not show personalised ads to users under 18. • We may use age checks and verification to enforce these rules and may restrict or remove accounts that breach them. Tips for young people: do not share your address, school or phone number with people you do not know; never send private photos; and tell a trusted adult, then report and block, if anyone makes you uncomfortable. Tips for parents and guardians: talk with your children about their activity, check their privacy settings, and contact us if you believe a child has accessed adult-only features or is being targeted. If you see content that endangers a child, report it immediately using the in-app reporting tool. You can also contact our child-safety team at facexo.workplace@gmail.com with the subject line CHILD SAFETY REPORT. This is the child-safety contact address published by FaceXO; it must be actively monitored by authorised personnel. Contact local law enforcement if a child is in immediate danger. 3. Sexual Content and Adult Material FaceXO is a social platform used by people of different ages and cultures. We keep sexual content off public areas of the platform. Not allowed: • Pornography and sexually explicit images, videos, audio or live broadcasts. • Nudity or sexually suggestive content in public posts, Reels, profile photos, comments or live broadcasts. • Non-consensual intimate content, including intimate images shared without the person’s consent, secretly recorded material, and threats to share such content (sometimes called “revenge porn” or sextortion). • Unsolicited sexual messages, images or requests sent to anyone. • Sexual content involving minors, in any form. See Section 2. • Sexual services, escorting, prostitution or solicitation, and promotion of sexual services. • Sexual content that is exploitative, coercive or involves violence or force. • Content that links to, advertises or promotes adult websites or services. Limited exceptions: we may allow content that is genuinely educational, medical, artistic, journalistic or related to breastfeeding or health awareness, as long as it is not sexualised and is appropriately presented. These exceptions may apply to public posts, Reels and live broadcasts where appropriate. We may restrict such content with warnings or limit who sees it. These exceptions do not permit sexual content involving minors. Consensual adult conversations between adults in private messages are subject to these Guidelines and applicable law. Users must not send sexual content to anyone under 18, and adults must not use FaceXO messaging, voice notes or calls to involve minors in sexual conversations or activities. We may restrict or remove content and accounts where we identify such conduct. Users should report unsolicited sexual content immediately. 4. Dating Safety and Consent The dating features are optional and for adults aged 18 and above only. They are separate from your ordinary social activity, and we do not publish dating information to your normal feed. Be honest and respectful: • Use your own real photographs and accurate information about your age, identity and intentions. • Do not use another person’s photos or identity, or run more than one dating profile to deceive people. • Respect consent and boundaries. A “no”, an unmatch, a block or silence means stop. • Do not pressure, guilt, threaten or coerce anyone for contact, photographs, money or meetings. • Do not send unsolicited sexual content. • Do not discriminate against, mock or abuse people because of who they are. Not allowed: • Romance scams or asking for money, gifts, airtime, mobile money or financial help from matches. • Using dating features for trafficking, prostitution, solicitation or commercial exploitation. • Catfishing, which means pretending to be someone else to deceive or exploit. • Stalking or tracking a match, or trying to find out where they live or work after they have declined contact. • Recording, screenshotting or sharing someone’s dating profile or private conversations to humiliate or expose them. • Being married or in a relationship while hiding it in order to deceive a match, where it is used to manipulate or exploit. Users should report suspected underage dating profiles or anyone they believe is misrepresenting their age to access dating features. Stay safe when meeting someone: • Take your time before sharing personal details, and never send money to someone you have not met and trust. • Do not share your home address, workplace, identity documents or financial details. • Meet in a public place, tell a friend or family member where you are going, and arrange your own transport. • Trust your instincts, and leave if you feel uncomfortable or unsafe. • Report anything suspicious using the reporting tools. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. FaceXO does not run a full background check on every user, so please use caution. We may suspend or remove dating access for users who breach these rules or pose a safety risk. 5. Scams, Impersonation and Fraudulent Accounts Trust is essential to FaceXO. We take action against deception and fraud. Not allowed: • Scams of any kind, including romance scams, advance-fee schemes, fake job or loan offers, fake giveaways and prizes, investment and “quick money” schemes, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, and fake charity appeals. • Phishing, or tricking people into sharing passwords, one-time codes, PINs, mobile money or banking details, or identity documents. • Impersonating another person, business, public figure, organisation or institution, including by using their name, photos or branding to mislead. • Pretending to be FaceXO staff or claiming to speak for FaceXO. • Fake, duplicate or automated accounts, accounts created to evade a ban, and accounts that use stolen or borrowed identities or verification information. • Selling, buying, renting or trading accounts, usernames or verification status. • Misleading others about who you are, your age, your location or your intentions in order to exploit them. • Sharing malicious links, malware or content designed to steal data or take over accounts. • Pretending that an account is verified or has been endorsed when it has not. Parody and fan accounts: these are allowed only if clearly labelled as such and not intended to deceive. Protect yourself: FaceXO will never ask for your password. Never share your one-time codes, and do not send money to people you do not know. A verification badge, where shown, confirms only that a verification step was completed. It is not a guarantee of a person’s character or honesty. 6. Marketplace Prohibited Products and Conduct The Marketplace helps people discover products and contact sellers. At first launch, FaceXO does not process payments, hold funds or guarantee transactions, and deals happen between users outside the app. You must not list, promote or sell: • Stolen, counterfeit, pirated, fake or misrepresented goods. • Illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, or prescription and controlled medicines without proper legal authorisation. • Weapons, firearms, ammunition, explosives, or items designed to harm people. • Human beings, human organs, body parts or fluids, or anything connected with trafficking or exploitation. • Endangered or protected animals, wildlife products, and products made from them in breach of law. • Adult services, sexual content or products, or escorting. • Hazardous, unsafe or recalled items, or items that cannot lawfully be sold. • Counterfeit currency, forged documents, stolen identity data, hacked accounts or illegal services. • Items or services that require a licence, permit or professional registration that the seller does not hold. • Alcohol, tobacco or age-restricted products in ways that breach the law or target minors. • Misleading offers, bait-and-switch listings, deposit scams or items that do not exist. • Fake reviews, fake ratings or manipulated seller reputations. All Marketplace listings and transactions must comply with applicable Zambian laws and any other laws that apply to the transaction. Seller standards: use accurate descriptions, real photographs of the item, honest prices and clear conditions and defects, and respond truthfully to enquiries. Buyer and seller safety: • Inspect items before paying where possible. • Meet in busy public places and bring a friend if you can. • Do not pay in advance to someone you do not know and trust. • Be wary of deals that seem too good to be true, requests to move to another platform, or pressure to act fast. • Report suspicious listings and sellers. FaceXO does not inspect or certify every listing, so please use your judgement. We remove listings and may restrict sellers who breach these rules. 7. Copyright and Intellectual Property Respect the work of creators. Only post what you own or have permission to share. Not allowed: • Uploading films, television programmes, music, sound recordings, photographs, artwork, books or other protected material you do not have the right to share. • Re-uploading other people’s videos, Reels or live broadcasts as your own without permission. • Using copyrighted music or sound in Reels, videos or live broadcasts without the right to do so. • Selling or promoting counterfeit goods or using brands in a way that misleads people. • Recording, copying or redistributing XO TV and XO Movies content, or circumventing content protection. • Using another person’s name, image, voice or likeness in a misleading or harmful way. Giving credit is not the same as having permission. If you are unsure, do not post it. If you are a rights holder: if you believe content on FaceXO infringes your rights, send us your name and contact details, a description of the protected work, the location of the content (such as a link or username), a statement that you own or are authorised to act for the owner, and a statement that your information is accurate. Send your notice to facexo.workplace@gmail.com or use any copyright-reporting form we make available on our website. We may remove the content and notify the user. Repeat infringers may lose their accounts, and knowingly false claims may lead to account action. 8. Spam, Fake Engagement and Manipulation Genuine interaction keeps FaceXO useful and fair. Not allowed: • Spam, including repeated, unwanted or irrelevant messages, comments, tags, mentions or links, and mass or unsolicited promotions. • Buying, selling, trading or exchanging followers, likes, views, comments, matches or reactions. • Using bots, scripts, auto-follow tools, click farms or other automated methods to inflate engagement, create accounts or scrape data without our written permission. • Coordinated activity designed to mislead others about popularity, such as networks of fake or duplicate accounts. • Clickbait, misleading captions or content designed to trick people into clicking, following or sharing. • Posting the same content repeatedly across feeds, groups or comment sections to dominate attention. • Interfering with advertising, including clicking ads artificially or encouraging others to do so. • Circumventing limits, restrictions or suspensions, or exploiting bugs and weaknesses in the platform. • Collecting other users’ information or building lists of users. • Chain messages and “share to win” content that is deceptive. Advertising and promotion: commercial content must be honest and clearly identified as promotional where appropriate, and must follow the Marketplace and advertising rules in our Terms. Official ads on FaceXO are shown through our advertising partners. We may remove content, remove engagement we believe is not genuine, reduce reach, and restrict or suspend accounts that manipulate the platform. 9. Live-Streaming Rules Live broadcasting is happening in real time, so responsibility is high. You are responsible for everything shown and said in your broadcast, including by people who appear with you. Before you go live: • Make sure you have the rights to everything you broadcast, including music and other material. • Get consent from people who appear clearly in your broadcast where consent is required. • Remember that anyone who watches can see what you share and may record or capture it outside FaceXO. • Avoid sharing private information, such as your address, identity documents, passwords or children’s details. Not allowed in live broadcasts: • Anything prohibited elsewhere in these Guidelines, including nudity, sexual activity, graphic violence, hate, harassment, threats, scams and illegal activity. • Live content that shows or promotes self-harm, suicide, dangerous stunts or serious harm to people or animals. • Broadcasting minors in ways that sexualise, exploit or endanger them. • Promoting illegal sales, gambling scams, fake giveaways or fraud. • Broadcasting someone without their knowledge in a private place, or filming people in distress for entertainment. • Using someone else’s copyrighted content, such as films, matches or other broadcasts, without permission. • Misleading titles, thumbnails or descriptions that do not reflect what is in the broadcast. Third-party delivery: live broadcasts are delivered through YouTube, so YouTube’s policies and technical systems may also apply, and a broadcast could be restricted or removed if YouTube takes action. Our right and ability to act: we may review reports and available broadcast information, warn the broadcaster, restrict the broadcast, suspend live access or take the broadcast offline where these Guidelines or the Terms are breached. FaceXO will maintain administrative procedures and access controls so authorised moderators can escalate urgent incidents and request or initiate interruption or termination of a broadcast as quickly as reasonably practicable. Because broadcasts are delivered through YouTube, the exact method and speed of stopping a stream may depend on the controls and response available through YouTube and our integration. We will use the available platform and provider controls to address serious or urgent violations. We do not guarantee that every broadcast can be stopped instantaneously, but we will act promptly once a serious violation is identified or reported. Private calls: one-to-one voice and video calls are for the people on them. Do not use calls to harass, threaten, scam or pressure anyone, and do not record or share someone’s call without any consent that the law requires. FaceXO does not record or store the content of one-to-one calls. 10. Reporting, Blocking and Enforcement Reporting If you see something that breaks these Guidelines, please report it using the in-app reporting tools. You can report posts, comments, individual messages or conversations, accounts, Reels, live broadcasts, Marketplace listings, dating profiles and advertisements, where the relevant reporting option is available in the app. When a private message or conversation is reported, authorised FaceXO reviewers may examine the reported message and relevant surrounding context to assess the report, investigate suspected abuse, protect users and enforce these Guidelines. Private messages are not treated as public content, and access to reported message content is limited to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes and permitted by law. Users should include the relevant message or conversation details when contacting us about a messaging concern. • Reports are reviewed using a combination of user reports, human review and automated tools. • Provide as much detail as you can, such as what happened and where. • Do not make knowingly false, malicious or repeated reports. Abuse of reporting tools may lead to action against your account. • We prioritise reports according to the seriousness and urgency of the risk. Reports involving suspected child sexual exploitation, grooming, threats of violence, trafficking, imminent danger or other serious safety risks receive priority handling. Other reports are reviewed as soon as reasonably practicable, taking account of their nature, volume and complexity. • We may take temporary protective action while a report is being assessed, including restricting content, limiting an account or interrupting a live broadcast where our systems allow. • We do not promise a fixed response time for every report. We aim to review reports promptly and will take appropriate action where a breach is identified. • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. FaceXO cannot act as an emergency service. Blocking and your controls You can protect yourself using the controls available in FaceXO: • Block a user so they cannot directly interact with you through the account-blocking features available in the app. • Report content or people that concern you using the reporting options available in the app. • Manage who can see your profile and content using the privacy settings provided in the app, and control notifications. • Manage or turn off dating features where this control is available. • Control camera, microphone and location permissions in your device settings. Advertising preferences may also be available through your device or the relevant advertising provider. The availability and names of particular controls may vary by app version, device or feature. FaceXO will not represent a control as available unless it has been implemented. If you cannot find an appropriate in-app reporting or safety control, contact us using the details in Section 11. These Guidelines do not replace the need for FaceXO to provide the reporting, blocking and moderation features required for the services it offers. Blocking is designed to prevent direct interactions between accounts, but it may not prevent someone from viewing content that is publicly available through other accounts, external websites or copies shared outside FaceXO. Blocking and reporting are intended to be fast and confidential from the other person’s point of view. How we enforce these Guidelines We consider the seriousness of the violation, the potential for harm, whether it is repeated, any context, applicable law and the information available to us. Depending on the case, we may: • Remove or restrict content, or reduce its reach. • Add a warning or age or sensitivity restriction to content. • Send a notice or warning to the user. • Limit features, such as posting, messaging, going live, Marketplace selling or dating access. • Remove Marketplace listings or restrict sellers. • Temporarily suspend an account. • Permanently terminate an account, especially for serious or repeated violations. • Preserve information and report suspected criminal activity, including child sexual abuse material, to the authorities. Some violations, such as child exploitation, serious threats, trafficking and serious fraud, may lead to immediate and permanent action without warning. For less serious or first-time issues, we may use warnings or temporary limits first. Where appropriate, enforcement notices will explain the action taken and provide information about how to request a review. We may limit the information provided where disclosure could compromise an investigation, another person's privacy or platform security. Appeals and review If you think we made a mistake, you can ask us to review our decision by contacting us at the email address in Section 11, explaining what happened and why you believe the decision was wrong. We will consider reasonable requests, although we are not required to reverse a decision, and we may not be able to restore content in cases involving serious safety, legal or security risks. We aim to review appeals as soon as reasonably practicable. Review times may vary depending on the seriousness and complexity of the matter and the information available. Evading enforcement Creating new accounts to avoid a suspension or ban, or helping others to do so, is a serious violation and may lead to permanent removal. 11.
